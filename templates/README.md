# Research System Templates

This is the single maintained template area for the research system.

Do not create new reusable templates under `daily_research_reports/`, `docs/`,
`paper_reading/`, or project folders by default. If a template becomes reusable,
put it in the right subfolder here and link to it from the relevant skill,
prompt, or README.

## Folder Map

| Folder | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `research_process/` | Templates for starting, structuring, or continuing a research iteration. |
| `reporting/` | Templates for advisor, collaborator, meeting, or sync-facing report cards. |
| `reading/` | Templates for paper, blog, and reading-note work. |
| `meeting/` | Templates for private meeting preparation, notes, and recordings. |
| `archive/` | Historical templates kept for compatibility only. |

## Research Process Templates

| Template | Use |
|---|---|
| `research_process/minimal_anchor_handwrite.md` | Minimal hand-written anchor draft for user thinking before AI expansion. |
| `research_process/research_loop_folder_template.md` | Standard daily research loop folder structure after a topic becomes an actual iteration. |

## Reporting Templates

Use these when compressing already-updated anchor / summary / detailed evidence
for advisor, collaborator, meeting, or sync reading.

| Template | Use |
|---|---|
| `reporting/report_card_template.md` | Advisor-facing report card after an experiment closure. Save as `05_report_card.md`. |
| `reporting/deprecated_reporting_card_template.md` | Compatibility note only. Use `reporting/report_card_template.md`. |

## Reading Templates

Reading templates do not replace research anchors.

| Template | Use |
|---|---|
| `reading/paper_reading_record.md` | Full paper reading record. |
| `reading/paper_blog_reading_record.md` | Blog or informal technical article reading record. |
| `reading/paper_summary_card.md` | Short reading summary card. |

## Meeting / Recording Templates

These are private preparation and record-keeping surfaces. They should not be
shown as advisor-facing report cards unless explicitly rewritten for that
purpose.

| Template | Use |
|---|---|
| `meeting/meeting_brief_template.md` | Pre-meeting brief that states conclusion, minimum evidence, claim boundary, and next decision. Save as `meeting_brief.md`. |
| `meeting/meeting_notes_template.md` | Guidance plus lightweight structure for private post-meeting notes. |
| `meeting/recordings_template.md` | Meeting or discussion recording notes. |

## Archive

| Template | Status |
|---|---|
| `archive/daily_card_template_0519.md` | Historical daily-card backup. |
| `archive/daily_card_template_0521.md` | Historical daily-card backup. |

## Rules

- Report cards are saved as `05_report_card.md` inside the relevant daily,
  experiment, or sync package.
- Daily notes should stay minimal and free-form; do not recreate a daily-card
  template system.
- Historical archive snapshots may contain old template files. Do not use them
  as current templates.
