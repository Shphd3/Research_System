# Research Mainline

Use this skill to decide whether a result, reading note, experiment, or discussion should update the research mainline.

## Required Inputs

- Read `CURRENT.md` first when available.
- Read the user's current daily material or report.
- Treat remote experiment code and logs as evidence sources, not as the main memory.

## Decision Test

A material updates the mainline only if it changes at least one of these:

- the core hypothesis
- the mathematical/modeling definition
- the empirical support for or against the hypothesis
- a limitation that changes what can be claimed
- the next most important decision

If none of these changes, write: `No mainline update today`.

## Output Shape

Keep the answer short:

1. One-sentence conclusion
2. Mainline update: yes/no
3. What changed
4. What cannot be claimed
5. Next decision

Avoid praising novelty. Focus on whether the evidence moves the research forward.
