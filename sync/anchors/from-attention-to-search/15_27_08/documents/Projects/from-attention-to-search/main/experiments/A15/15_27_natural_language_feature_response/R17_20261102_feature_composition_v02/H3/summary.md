# H3: Equal-budget cross-frequency mixture

Protocol result branch: `COMPLETED_NOT_SUPPORTED`. Scientific acceptance and next decision: `Unknown`. Corrected output reuses an exposed holdout. The original imported hypothesis is unchanged.

## Purpose and hypothesis

Test the declared positive dual advantage of a mixed-frequency group under equal16-member budget. High-only sufficiency is the main rival.

## Experiment and analysis

## Conclusion, hypothesis revision and AI suggestion

The positive dual advantage is not supported in this design. The mixed arm beats M but loses to H; retain both comparisons. Do not generalize to all targets or learning. AI suggests target-specific conditional complementarity rather than a universal advantage of frequency mixing.
