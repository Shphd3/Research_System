# How might this have happened?

Status: AI_CONJECTURE. The [analysis](result_analysis.md) supplies observations, not confirmed causes or researcher acceptance.

**Conjecture1: learned maps remain bound to prompt format, without a callable interface for composition.** Original-format atomic fitting, poor changed-format access and perfect explicit two-call composition fit this account. If correct, improving atomic consistency across surfaces without adding composition labels should make later matched-supervision composition and revision transfer more attainable. A live rival is extra serial computation from two calls: improved format transfer may still leave single-forward composition poor. Cross-format competence is not sufficient proof.

**Conjecture2: the selected update location and direct-answer supervision favor fitting whole pair-input maps over stable serial computation.** The first27 blocks are fixed. A final block can fit seen questions while failing held-out composition; composition teaching has no atomic rehearsal and original atomic behavior also drops. A wider adapter does not add serial execution steps. If this limitation matters, a controlled change in interface or trainable depth should alter held-out behavior and directed interventions under matched supervision. Optimization, finite coverage and arbitrary permutations remain rivals. No experiment here independently varied these factors, so final-block causation is unproven.

These accounts can coexist. The evidence-near working hypothesis is: establish callable component competence across contexts before testing whether rare compositions call it. This does not assert that format generalization guarantees composition.

AI proposed next decision: whether to make cross-context callable components a reliable prerequisite and then test transfer after qualification. This is a proposed scientific choice, not another run, a replacement for R01, or a change to parallel order V2.
