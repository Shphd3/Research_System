## Choose a reading surface

- Named result: start at its experiment Summary, then read Detailed for evidence.
- Named question: use the [Focus index](../../../../UNAVAILABLE.md), its Anchor and Protocol. A registered owner without a Focus retains its identity without being activated by this index.
- Design or old identifier: use the [complete Anchor index](../../../../UNAVAILABLE.md), [ownership registry](../../../../sources/Projects/from-attention-to-search/main/anchor_ownership.json) and [migration record](../../../../UNAVAILABLE.md).
- Data, supporting material or synthesis: use the [dataset registry](../../../../UNAVAILABLE.md), [research notes](../../../../UNAVAILABLE.md) and [Stories](../../../../UNAVAILABLE.md); these do not replace formal owners.

[System PROJECTS.md](../../../../UNAVAILABLE.md) selects a project. This roadmap navigates inside it. Scientific conclusions and next decisions stay in the owning Focus / Anchor, never in list order or folder existence.
