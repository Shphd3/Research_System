## Choose a reading surface

- Named result: start at its experiment Summary, then read Detailed for evidence.
- Named question: use the [Focus index](focus/README.md), its Anchor and Protocol. A registered owner without a Focus retains its identity without being activated by this index.
- Design or old identifier: use the [complete Anchor index](problem_anchors/README.md), [ownership registry](anchor_ownership.json) and [migration record](organization_migrations/20261022/README.md).
- Data, supporting material or synthesis: use the [dataset registry](datasets/README.md), [research notes](research_notes/) and [Stories](stories/); these do not replace formal owners.

[System PROJECTS.md](../../../PROJECTS.md) selects a project. This roadmap navigates inside it. Scientific conclusions and next decisions stay in the owning Focus / Anchor, never in list order or folder existence.
