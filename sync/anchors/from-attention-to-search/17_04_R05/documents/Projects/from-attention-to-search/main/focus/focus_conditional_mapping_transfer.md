## Question and boundary

This sibling question studies the relation between tasks. It does not repeat A17_03's intervention on update components. ## Purpose and completion

Both old commands are supervised: one selects the first list item and one the last. New-mode inputs are identical across the consistent and reversed experimental worlds; their answer mapping differs. This tests conditional mapping change, not whether teaching a missing position completes prior position knowledge. Completion means frozen data/contracts, qualified Smoke and exact external-process resume, all specified training branches and readouts, actual saved-asset registration, and bilingual factual results. It does not require a negative transfer result or a favorable mechanism interpretation.

## Analysis steps

1. Learn both commands in mode one, then branch from the same old endpoint to consistent and reversed mappings in mode two with matched exposure and budget.
2. Compare old answer advantage, loss and accuracy on separate unseen-order/unseen-combination panels and their equal-weight combination. Separate candidate probability changes from relative rule-answer preference; compare the two mode responses.
3. Check equal-norm immediate effects, comparable new-task learning levels and a joint reversed-mapping feasibility control. Preserve difficulty, learning-path and capacity uncertainty when those checks fail.
