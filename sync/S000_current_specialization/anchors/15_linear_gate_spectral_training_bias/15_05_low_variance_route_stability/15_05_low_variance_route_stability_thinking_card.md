# Thinking Card: Low-Variance Routing Stability

The researcher conjectures that routing from low-variance directions may be as
unstable as routing from a random basis. AI operationalization defines
stability functionally: a route must be both correct and persistent. The same
balanced label is encoded in equal-dimensional H/M/T blocks at matched
standardized signal-to-noise ratio; only block variance changes. Equalized
variance and label-free random subspaces distinguish finite-time variance
effects from random routing.
