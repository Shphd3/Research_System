Phenomenon: Natural text contains explicit superordinate--subordinate chains such as mathematics--algebra--linear algebra, with coarse and fine concepts in one sentence.

Mechanism judgment: concept granularity may systematically change which covariance-ranked directions are activated.

Primary uncertainty: any observed contrast may instead come from lexical identity, tokenization length, position, or surrounding syntax.
