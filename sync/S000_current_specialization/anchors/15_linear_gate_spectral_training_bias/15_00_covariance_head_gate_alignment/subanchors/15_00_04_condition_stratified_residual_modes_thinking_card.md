# A15_00_04 Thinking Card: Condition-Stratified Residual Modes

- **Current judgment:** Random document groups mix domains and can average condition-varying low-dimensional modes into an apparently stable residual subspace.
- **Mechanism:** Residual projectors should overlap more between independent halves of the same domain/topic than across domains/topics, while cross-condition bases may still capture energy when all modes lie in a shared residual envelope.
- **Strongest rival:** The residual projector is genuinely global across semantic conditions. A second rival is estimator noise, which predicts low within- and cross-condition overlap.
- **Evidence that changes the judgment:** Joint measurement of same-condition half-split overlap, cross-condition overlap, and cross-capture against label permutations, matched random groups, and complement-Haar controls.
- **Next decision:** Adopt the conditional-mode interpretation only for a high-within/lower-cross overlap pattern with cross-capture above the matched null.
