# Thinking Card: Can Dynamic Spectral Relay Produce Useful Routing?

- **Phenomenon:** A linear Gate has a controlled covariance-head learning-speed bias. Frozen-band hard switching caused a large adaptation cost, while a block-1 Relay showed only a small late 2B endpoint advantage.
- **Mechanism judgment:** Dynamically updated, continuity-constrained bands may reduce stale-basis and hard-switch costs; a shared block-1 source may be easier to search than separately drifting local layer coordinates.
- **Strongest rival:** Any Relay benefit may come from an arbitrary stable bottleneck, centering, load changes, or extra statistical compute rather than covariance H/M/T identity.
- **Largest uncertainty:** Whether covariance identity yields a 10B endpoint NLL benefit that an equal-dimension random Relay cannot reproduce.
- **Judgment-changing evidence:** Relay-HMT beats Centered-Full, Local-HMT, and Relay-Random in both seeds with paired-document support and without a load or compute explanation.
- **Current next decision:** Run five matched arms at two seeds for 10B tokens per job and decide by 10B held-out NLL.

This card preserves the approved human judgment and does not assign universal semantic meaning to H/M/T.
