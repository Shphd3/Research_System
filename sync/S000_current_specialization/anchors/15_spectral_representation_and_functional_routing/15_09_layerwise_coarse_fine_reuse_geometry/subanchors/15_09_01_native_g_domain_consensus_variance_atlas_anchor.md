# A15_09_01: Native-Router Domain Consensus And Variance Atlas


## 1. Problem Definition

The decision question is: at Block 46 of frozen Qwen3-30B-A3B, do the eight domain-local rank-seven child-label discriminant spaces of the native Router input $G=X+U$ share stable label-dependent input directions beyond a matched label-permutation rival, and where do any admitted consensus directions lie in the raw same-layer variance spectrum?

This is the smallest unresolved explanation of the prior retrospective result: individual domains required about six or seven functional dimensions while one eight-domain shared space required 16, not the sum of 53 local dimensions. Direction reuse could explain that compression, but the prior overlap diagnostic used standardized coordinates, fixed rank seven, and no matched semantic null. A Pass permits only a frozen retain/remove intervention on the admitted consensus directions. A Fail stops the stable-reuse explanation. Insufficient evidence leaves the E06 compression unexplained. Native Router use, expert utility, and training gain are out of scope.

## 2. Physical Priors

The sole prior is that genuine cross-domain reuse must survive two controls: each domain's discriminant space must be reproducible under template-family perturbation, and cross-domain overlap must exceed overlap produced when child labels are permuted while the same representations, domains, templates, estimator, and ranks are retained. Covariance-driven or unstable directions can exceed a uniform-random geometric reference but should not pass this matched semantic comparison.

## 3. Falsifiable Hypotheses

The primary hypothesis is that Block 46 contains stable label-dependent directions reused across the eight domain-local discrimination tasks. It predicts that all eight local spaces pass rank and same-task reproducibility guards and that their mean pairwise projector overlap exceeds the 97.5th percentile of the matched label-permutation distribution.

The strongest rival is that the apparent overlap is generated by shared covariance, feature standardization, or finite-sample estimation. It predicts that the observed overlap does not exceed the matched null or that the local spaces fail reproducibility. The former is Fail; the latter is Insufficient rather than evidence of orthogonality.

## 4. Mathematical Model

For block $\ell$ and domain $d$, let $Q_{\ell,d}\in\mathbb R^{2048\times7}$ be the Euclidean-orthonormal raw-coordinate input space obtained from the rank-seven child-label coefficient matrix. For two domains,

$$
\Omega_{\ell}(d,e)=\frac{\lVert Q_{\ell,d}^{\top}Q_{\ell,e}\rVert_F^2}{7}.
$$

The primary observable is the equal-pair mean $T_{46}$ over all 28 domain pairs. The average projector

$$
\bar P_{\ell}=\frac{1}{8}\sum_{d=1}^{8}Q_{\ell,d}Q_{\ell,d}^{\top}
$$

defines consensus directions through its eigenvectors. Its eigenvalues measure average membership across domain spaces; they do not by themselves establish common label semantics or native model use.

For raw TRAIN covariance eigenvectors $V_{\ell,b}$ spanning variance band $b$, band enrichment is

$$
E_{\ell,b}=\frac{\operatorname{tr}(Q_{\ell}^{\top}V_{\ell,b}V_{\ell,b}^{\top}Q_{\ell})/r}{\dim(V_{\ell,b})/2048}.
$$

$E_{\ell,b}>1$ means geometric enrichment relative to a uniform subspace of the same rank. It is secondary and cannot rescue a failed label-permutation comparison.

## 5. Computational Realization

The frozen source contains 19,200 rows and all 48 blocks of $(X,U)$ at the final decision token, width 2048. The only analyzed representation is $G=X+U$. TRAIN has 11,520 rows; DEVELOPMENT, same-template TEST, and new-template TEST each have 2,560 rows. There are eight balanced parent domains and five template families overall; only the three same-template families occur in TRAIN, while the two new-template families occur only in new-template TEST.

At each layer, TRAIN-only coordinate means and scales reproduce the E06 standardization. Eight separate reduced-rank ridge coefficients distinguish the eight children inside each known parent. DEVELOPMENT selects the ridge value before any TEST use. Coefficient spaces are mapped back to raw $G$ coordinates with the inverse TRAIN scale and re-orthonormalized before overlap or covariance comparison.

Block 46 is the only verdict-bearing layer because it was selected before this analysis by E06. All 48 layers form a secondary atlas under the same frozen estimator. Matched label permutations occur within each domain and TRAIN template family. Three leave-one-TRAIN-template-family-out fits measure same-task reproducibility without using TEST. Principal-component bands are defined from raw TRAIN covariance by cumulative variance: head $[0,50\%]$, middle $(50\%,90\%]$, and tail $(90\%,100\%]$.

## 6. Minimal Falsification Tests

The decisive comparison is observed $T_{46}$ versus 200 matched label-permutation replicates. Pass requires: every observed local coefficient has numerical rank seven; every domain's leave-one-family-out reproducibility exceeds its matched permutation 95th percentile; all identities are finite; and $T_{46}$ exceeds the permutation 97.5th percentile. Fail requires all validity guards to pass and $T_{46}$ not to exceed that percentile. Any failed validity guard yields Insufficient.

The 48-layer overlap curve, consensus spectrum, principal-angle components, variance-band enrichment, and existing DEVELOPMENT readability curve are secondary explanations. Layerwise excess is reported with a permutation max-over-layers reference; no visually selected layer can change the Block 46 verdict.

## 7. Current Evidence

The predecessor E06 found retrospective same-template $G$ local functional ranks of six or seven, sum 53, and a directly learned shared functional rank of 16, but no shared confirmation rank. Its secondary standardized-coordinate diagnostic at Block 46 reported mean pairwise overlap 0.04184 across 28 pairs, while average same-task bootstrap overlap was 0.47829. Because no matched label-permutation comparison or raw-coordinate variance localization was performed, stable label-dependent reuse remains NOT RUN. The auditable predecessor evidence is in [E06 Summary](/Projects/from-attention-to-search/main/experiments/A15/15_08_target_conditioned_layer_innovation/A15_08_06_E06_rank_specific_multirepresentation_shared_scaling/summary.md).

## 8. Claim Boundary And Next Decision

Before this experiment, stable cross-domain reuse and variance location are unresolved. Even a Pass can establish only that supervised linear child-label directions overlap beyond the matched null in this frozen representation and dataset. It cannot establish shared label meaning, causal necessity, native Router use, expert utility, nonlinear structure, or training benefit.

Exactly one next decision follows: whether Block 46 consensus directions are admitted into a frozen retain/remove intervention. Completion requires an eligible Block 46 verdict, audited central figures, and a human-confirmed result boundary; the resume action is to write that intervention Anchor only after a Pass.
