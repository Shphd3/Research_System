# A15_09: Layerwise Coarse--Fine Discriminant-Space Reuse Geometry

Thinking Card source: [15_09_layerwise_coarse_fine_reuse_geometry_thinking_card_cn.md](15_09_layerwise_coarse_fine_reuse_geometry_thinking_card_cn.md)

## 1. Problem Definition

The decision question is: in the frozen native Router/expert input of
Qwen3-30B-A3B, how much do the parent-conditional mathematics and physics
fine-class Fisher discriminant spaces reuse the eight-parent coarse-class
Fisher space at each of six fixed depths, and how does that relation change
across depth?

Reuse means normalized projector overlap between stable rank-seven input
spaces. It excludes native Router use, expert utility, causal necessity, and
training gain. The smallest direction-changing unknown is whether a shared
coarse space, a fine residual space, or layer-dependent geometry is the least
contradicted premise for a later causal architecture test.

## 2. Physical Priors

The sole prior is that if hierarchical semantic distinctions are organized
differently across network depth, their stable supervised input-space
discriminants should show a reproducible depth-dependent overlap pattern. The
prior is weakened when C4 and C5 disagree after rank and stability guards, and
is not testable at layers whose spaces are rank-insufficient or unstable.

## 3. Falsifiable Hypotheses

The primary empirical proposition is that at least one target parent has a
coarse--fine overlap trajectory that is reproducible across the independently
constructed C4 and C5 expression panels. Reproducible trajectories may be
monotone, stage-structured, or non-monotone; monotonicity is not assumed.

The strongest rival is that apparent layer differences are generated by
finite-sample subspace instability, the common Fisher metric, or expression
templates. Rank gates, split-half reconstruction, matched label-permutation
nulls, and C5 replication separate this rival. Failure to replicate is not
evidence of depth invariance; it is only failure to establish a stable depth
pattern.

## 4. Mathematical Model

For panel $p$, block $\ell$, and fixed half $s\in\{A,B\}$, let
$S_{C,p,\ell}^{s}$ be the rank-seven Fisher space separating all eight parents
and $S_{F\mid c,p,\ell}^{s}$ the rank-seven space separating the eight children
inside parent $c\in\{\mathrm{mathematics},\mathrm{physics}\}$. For orthonormal
bases $U,V$, define

$$
\Omega(U,V)=\frac{\lVert U^\top V\rVert_F^2}{7}.
$$

The cross-half raw alignment is

$$
A_{c,p,\ell}=\frac{1}{2}\left[
\Omega(S_{C,p,\ell}^{A},S_{F\mid c,p,\ell}^{B})+
\Omega(S_{C,p,\ell}^{B},S_{F\mid c,p,\ell}^{A})
\right].
$$

Let $R_C$ and $R_F$ be the corresponding same-task A/B overlaps. The empirical
reconstruction reference is $C_{c,p,\ell}=\sqrt{R_C R_F}$ and the corrected
quantity is $A^{\mathrm{corr}}=A/C$. This correction is an empirical stability
ratio, not a proven unbiased estimator; it may exceed one and is never clipped.

## 5. Computational Realization

The model is frozen Qwen3-30B-A3B with 48 blocks and hidden width 2048. The
measured tensor is the final decision-token vector actually passed into each
block's MoE Gate and experts. Blocks 4, 12, 20, 28, 36, and 44 are fixed before
extraction. C4 is the primary panel and C5 is the independent replication
panel; both contain 8 parents by 8 children by 30 expression templates.

Each panel is split by whole template family into fixed balanced A/B halves.
Every layer uses the same pooled leaf-centered Ledoit--Wolf within-class metric.
The coarse space uses all parents; the two fine spaces use only their fixed
parent's eight children. No layer, parent, panel, threshold, or estimator may
be replaced after any scientific output is visible.

## 6. Minimal Falsification Tests

The decisive evidence is the complete six-layer corrected-overlap trajectory,
conditioned on rank and stability eligibility, replicated from C4 to C5.
Spearman correlation is a monotone-trend auxiliary only. Shallow, middle, and
deep stage means use fixed pairs $(4,12)$, $(20,28)$, and $(36,44)$; replicated
stage order and a replicated middle-stage maximum or minimum describe staged
or non-monotone structure without turning 0.25/0.75 into verdict thresholds.

Every layer retains raw alignment, label-permutation and Haar nulls, empirical
reconstruction reference, corrected alignment, and seven cross-half principal
angle $\cos^2$ values. An invalid layer remains in place and makes a complete
six-layer trend insufficient; it is never replaced.

## 7. Current Evidence

The known-truth synthetic calibration recovered rank, stability, and ordered
coarse--fine overlap under fixed rank-seven geometry. It established only that
the estimator can be used as a measurement gate.

The registered Qwen3-30B-A3B audit is now complete. All 24 C4/C5 by
mathematics/physics by block cells passed rank and A/B stability gates. Raw
overlap is 0.00649--0.03858, the empirical reconstruction reference is
0.09778--0.27062, and corrected overlap is 0.05964--0.14452. Every observed raw
value is below its matched label-permutation median, so the audit establishes
stable low overlap but no positive excess coarse--fine reuse.

Physics reproduces the descriptive shallow $>$ middle $>$ deep stage order;
mathematics does not reproduce the full ordering or monotone sign. No
middle-stage non-monotone extremum reproduces. The detailed evidence and
figures are recorded in [A15_09_E01](/Projects/from-attention-to-search/main/experiments/A15/15_09_layerwise_coarse_fine_reuse_geometry/A15_09_E01_qwen3_30b_cross_depth_coarse_fine_reuse/summary.md).

## 8. Claim Boundary And Next Decision

The audit establishes stable but null-subordinate coarse--fine overlap on the
controlled expression panels. It disfavours dominant shared-coarse geometry
for conditional fine discrimination and is most compatible with a
fine-residual premise. It does not establish native Router use, expert utility,
causal residual sufficiency, general natural-language universality, or
training benefit. The one next decision is whether conditional fine function
is retained in the coarse-space complement and specifically lost when the
fine-specific residual directions are removed. Router training is not
authorized by this anchor.
