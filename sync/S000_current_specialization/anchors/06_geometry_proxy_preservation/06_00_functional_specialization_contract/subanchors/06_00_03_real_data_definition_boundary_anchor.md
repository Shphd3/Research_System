# A06_00_03 Real-Data Definition Boundary

Thinking Card source: [06_00_functional_specialization_contract_thinking_card.md](../06_00_functional_specialization_contract_thinking_card.md)
Status: completed subanchor.

**Parent:** [`A06_00`](../06_00_functional_specialization_contract_anchor.md).

## Local Clause

A reproducible real-text partition may be used as a proxy for route
measurement, but it cannot be called specialization until experts have
different held-out utility on that partition.

## Falsifiable Question

Can split stability, linear routability, and balanced usage alone identify a
functionally meaningful real-data feature?

## Evidence And Verdict

The tested real-text proxy is reproducible and linearly routable, yet later
training overwrites its partition and no counterfactual expert advantage was
established. These measurements validate a proxy, not a useful expert role.

**Disposition:** keep as the real-data claim guard.

**Boundary:** semantic labels, domain names, and clusters are permitted for
stratified evaluation; none is a correct-expert label without a utility test.
