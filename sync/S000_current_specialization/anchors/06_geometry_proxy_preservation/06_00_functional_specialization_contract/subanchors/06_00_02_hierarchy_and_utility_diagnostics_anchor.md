# A06_00_02 Hierarchy And Utility Diagnostics

Status: completed subanchor.

**Parent:** [`A06_00`](../06_00_functional_specialization_contract_anchor.md).

## Local Clause

A route hierarchy is useful only when its branches correspond to stable
differences in expert utility, not merely to recoverable labels.

## Falsifiable Question

Does hierarchical route agreement imply that the selected expert minimizes
held-out loss for that condition?

## Rival Explanations

- The hierarchy reflects genuine conditional computation.
- The hierarchy reflects easy surface labels while experts remain
  functionally interchangeable.

## Evidence And Verdict

Existing hierarchical synthetic evidence shows that route identity and expert
utility can disagree. Therefore normalized mutual information and load balance
remain diagnostics, while routing regret is the decision metric.

**Disposition:** retain as the metric-separation guard for all later anchors.

**Boundary:** it does not prove that a real workload has expert utility
headroom.
