# A06_01_01 Common Geometry Nuisance Controls

Thinking Card source: [06_01_geometry_and_state_discovery_thinking_card.md](../06_01_geometry_and_state_discovery_thinking_card.md)
Status: completed subanchor.

**Parent:** [`A06_01`](../06_01_geometry_and_state_discovery_anchor.md).

## Local Clause

Common hidden-state directions and unequal Router-row norms can change expert
usage without identifying the routed feature.

## Falsifiable Question

Are observed load differences explained by feature alignment, or by common
components and row-norm amplification?

## Evidence And Verdict

Cross-checkpoint replay, freeze splits, Gaussian controls, hidden-state
decomposition, and centered residual diagnostics show that these nuisance
terms can reproduce usage concentration. Removing them improves geometric
interpretability but does not recover feature identity.

**Disposition:** retain as mandatory nuisance controls; park as a standalone
method direction.

**Boundary:** common removal cannot prove route relevance or expert utility.
