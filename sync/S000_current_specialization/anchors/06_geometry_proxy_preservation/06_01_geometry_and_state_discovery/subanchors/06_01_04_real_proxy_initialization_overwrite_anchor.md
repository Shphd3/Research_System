# A06_01_04 Real-Proxy Initialization Overwrite

Thinking Card source: [06_01_geometry_and_state_discovery_thinking_card.md](../06_01_geometry_and_state_discovery_thinking_card.md)
Status: completed subanchor with a negative preservation verdict.

**Parent:** [`A06_01`](../06_01_geometry_and_state_discovery_anchor.md).

## Local Clause

A real-text proxy can seed a nontrivial Router partition, but ordinary
language-model training may erase it before expert utility can bind.

## Falsifiable Question

Does a split-stable, linearly routable real proxy remain recoverable after the
first training steps?

## Evidence And Verdict

Held-out route agreement falls from 0.7549 at initialization to 0.0410 at step
5 and 0.0131 at step 10. The initialization therefore passes geometric
reproducibility but fails early preservation.

**Disposition:** retain as the real-proxy overwrite result; do not add more
initialization variants until a preservation mechanism is specified.

**Boundary:** the proxy has no established semantic or expert-utility status.
