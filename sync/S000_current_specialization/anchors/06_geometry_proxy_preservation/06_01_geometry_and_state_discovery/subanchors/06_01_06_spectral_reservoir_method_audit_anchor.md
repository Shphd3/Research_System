# A06_01_06 Spectral Reservoir Method Audit

Thinking Card source: [06_01_geometry_and_state_discovery_thinking_card.md](../06_01_geometry_and_state_discovery_thinking_card.md)
Status: completed method-falsification subanchor.

**Parent:** [`A06_01`](../06_01_geometry_and_state_discovery_anchor.md).

## Local Clause

Dominant or iteratively extracted expert directions are candidate Router rows
only if they distinguish routed conditions rather than reproduce common
energy.

## Falsifiable Question

Can singular-direction convergence, common-direction splitting, or iterative
reservoir extraction produce distinct feature-aware Router rows?

## Evidence And Verdict

The tested directions converge and can change forward routing, but remain
dominated by common geometry and do not establish condition-specific utility.
Iterative extraction does not convert spectral diversity into verified
functional specialization.

**Disposition:** merge into the geometry audit and park as a method family.

**Boundary:** spectral diversity is neither a feature label nor an expert
utility certificate.
