# V2.1 Claim Boundary

## Can claim after theory alone

- Recursive quantile splits give equal mass under population quantiles.
- Deterministic exact splitting is impossible for atoms larger than target mass.
- If drift is smaller than route margin, the route is preserved.

## Can claim only after experiments

- GA-SPR improves feature coherence.
- Expert-weight guidance improves routing.
- Margin-aware updates improve training preservation.
- Any method improves validation loss.

## Must not claim

- Covariance isotropy alone gives meaningful dispatch.
- Equal mass implies feature specialization.
- Expert weights always help.
- Synthetic results imply real LLM utility.
- Naive recalibration is safe.
