# Anti-Defensive Writing

State the finding, question or completed work directly, then give the evidence
and reasoning needed to understand it. Each paragraph develops one point.

## Apply the source rules

Read the relevant language and task section of the pinned
[upstream rules](references/upstream.md). They cover direct claims, necessary
precision, Chinese technical writing, unfavorable results and paper revision.
[Source and local adaptation](UPSTREAM.md) records the exact version and license.

Use precise scope instead of vague hedging. Keep a qualification when omitting
it would change the meaning, validity, evidence level or usable scope. Explain
shared limits once; keep a local qualifier where that claim needs it.

Remove sentences that add only imagined objections, repeated disclaimers,
apology-like framing or stock contrasts. Show the actual object, operation,
comparison and finding. Explain necessary terms at first use.

## Research_System integration

This is an editing helper. The existing Focus, Anchor, experiment or meeting
owner continues to determine purpose, evidence and scientific state. Use the
[shared writing contract](../../../docs/report_writing.md) and the requested
[result review mode](../../../docs/research_analysis_review_contract.md#review-mode).

- Keep values, conditions, sample units, uncertainty, adverse results and
  NOT RUN/BLOCKED/INVALID distinctions. Separate observation from explanation.
- A direct sentence cannot turn correlation into causation, numerical fit into
  a mechanism, or an unreviewed proposal into approval. Do not invent a paper
  contribution for an exploratory audit.
- Apply the shared review mode consistently across the reading layers. Preserve
  explicit fact-first requests; otherwise clear prose can present provisional
  AI explanations and rival predictions alongside the verified facts.
- Keep requested coverage complete. Consolidate repeated presentation and move
  full checks/logs to their owning record; do not remove experiments from scope.
- For AI work updates, say what changed or finished, its evidence and the next
  material step. Put routine implementation and full checks in linked records.
- Keep local edits local. Preserve frozen sources and recorded permissions;
  this skill creates no new execution, publication or approval step.

## Review the delivered passage

Can the reader identify the question or result, the decisive basis, and the
remaining uncertainty without reading the full audit history? Is every retained
qualification useful to that reading? Apply the same check to the actual text;
no fixed length, template count or rhetorical confidence substitutes for it.
