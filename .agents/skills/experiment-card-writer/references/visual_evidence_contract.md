# Visual Evidence Contract

Apply only when a figure/viewer materially helps inspect a claim. Text or a table
may be better. Do not create a viewer, service or extra review task by default.

Before plotting state the question, exact data/checkpoint, metric/unit,
aggregation/independence unit, axes/legend/conditions, scaling, expected pattern,
important alternative, observed-result source and maximum allowed conclusion.
Remove a plot with no distinct evidential question or label it debug-only.

A reader must find the setup, literal condition meanings, decisive value/pattern,
how to read the plot, narrow conclusion and uncertainty without private context.
Do not mix units on one axis or hide normalization. A schematic is labeled
illustrative, not measured evidence or a derivation of the result.

Inspect the actual rendered artifact for clipping, unreadable labels, hidden
scales, ambiguous conditions and unsupported conclusions; make concrete fixes
in a bounded cycle. If rendering is unavailable say unverified. Source/math
checks do not verify rendering. Do not delay direct results for visual polish.

Keep data/figure provenance and permitted date-local reading copies in the
existing evidence records. No raw-output promotion or automatic external serving.
