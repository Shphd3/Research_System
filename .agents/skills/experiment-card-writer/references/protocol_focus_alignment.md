# Align a Protocol with its Anchor

Give the experiment a stable name and link its owning Anchor and the specific question or hypothesis it tests. Link back to the experiment from the Anchor or its experiment index. Use the existing active-question entry or exploration log for context when needed.

Translate the chosen scientific design into a runnable comparison. State the intervention and control, the primary measurement and denominator, the stopping rule, and what supporting, non-supporting or insufficient evidence would change. Specify the project code version, command, inputs and intended execution workspace.

Use decisions already recorded in the Anchor or given by the researcher. When a material scientific choice is missing, mark the affected Protocol item as a draft and complete the grounded parts. A material revision reopens that choice. Formal execution follows the approved Protocol revision and its authorized resource scope.
