# Meeting Brief

Use for requested meeting preparation. Reuse the current Focus; never create a
Focus for a meeting, date, model switch, diagnostic or new result. One active
workstream has one current question; language companions are not new Focuses.

A requested file belongs at
`daily_research_reports/MMDD/docs/meeting_brief_<topic>[_cn].md`.
Use one brief per meeting/topic, with speaking text and optional backup in that
file. Reuse it during preparation. Do not generate a parallel slide deck,
long report, template or translation unless requested. Historical meeting files
remain references, not current research authority.

## Read And Select

Read the named Focus, relevant Summaries and existing mathematical bridge.
Open Detailed only for decisive setup, numbers or qualifications. The latest
qualified source owns the result, not an earlier chat summary. For a synthesis,
use the requested scope and make the relation to the current Focus explicit;
do not merge distinct experiments into a single verdict.

Choose the supported knowledge update before adding experiments. A missing
explanation needs clearer exposition; an untested relation needs a bounded
question. Negative or incomplete science can still be clearly presented.

## Speaking Order

Follow the user's timing and order. For the current three-minute format:

1. Problem and change in understanding: concrete data/model, long-term purpose,
   current question, and what this round resolved. No method inventory.
2. Mathematical/computational bridge: what is frozen or changed; how the input,
   prediction, loss and update produce the reported effect. Define symbols in
   words and distinguish exact relations from approximation.
3. Results and decision: at most two decisive comparisons, their qualifications,
   the bounded answer and one next decision. Keep unconfirmed choices labeled.

Put figures, detailed settings, earlier experiments and short interruption
answers after the main text. Each retained value/figure needs a source and a
reading sentence. Do not present a long Summary as the speaking script.

## Check Understanding

Can the researcher explain, without reading: what changes, why another datum
is affected, what was measured, and what does not follow? Practice an opening,
one derivation and interruption answers rather than memorizing the entire report.
If the explanation fails, repair the specific relation before adding material.

Mathematical identity is not empirical confirmation; attribution is not a separate
intervention; a skipped branch is not a negative experiment; one local effect is
not long-term shared/separate benefit. A failed reader does not prove information
absence. Preserve qualifications in the spoken conclusion, not only in backup.

A requested brief may be completed while the researcher's scientific judgment
is Unknown. It records sources and proposals; it does not confirm, close or
activate a question. Do not make meeting readiness depend on a new positive run.
