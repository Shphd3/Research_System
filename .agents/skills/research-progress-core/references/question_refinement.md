# Question Refinement And Evidence Propagation

Refine only when reasoning or evidence reveals a separable uncertainty, dependency,
coupling or boundary. A child needs its question, activation evidence, inherited
parent clause, reason its answer matters and bounded answer owed upward. Researcher
activation remains required. Inactive possibilities stay questions, not pre-filled
models, datasets, thresholds or directories.

Distinguish logical relations from filesystem ownership:
- `refines`: opens a part of the parent question;
- `depends-on`: needs another answer for interpretation;
- `coupled-with`: current evidence cannot identify the answers independently.

For materially coupled questions state the joint prediction, model/test, each
answer obligation, identifiability limit and evidence that would separate them.
Joint evidence does not independently prove every prior. Sibling dependency does
not make one question the other's child.

Propagate eligible result -> affected implementation/model/prior -> bounded answer
-> parent obligation and relevant relations -> proposed next decision. Preserve
measurement, operationalization and scientific failures at their actual level;
a failed proxy is not a universal refutation. Do not overwrite confirmed science
while performing a read-only audit.

One current Anchor owns the design, Detailed preserves complete rounds, and Focus
owns the current human decision. At closure retain the derived summary, boundary,
decision-changing findings and links; never delete or duplicate full evidence.
Use the existing Anchor placement contract; no parallel map, design or status tree.
