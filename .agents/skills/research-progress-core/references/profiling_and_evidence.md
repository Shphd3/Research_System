# Profiling And Evidence

Final result -> stage evidence -> supported mechanism or bottleneck -> bounded
update. For each consequential stage record its question, actual input/output,
accepted/rejected counts or conditions, failure reasons, inspectable artifact and
interpretation. Link to the mathematical item it tests. Do not require telemetry
that neither separates an alternative nor protects the claim.

Separate one-observation evidence, change across updates/conditions and aggregate
evidence. A static proxy cannot replace a trajectory or before/after comparison.
Use a trusted reference to inspect true/false positives and negatives, their costs
and discriminating variables, not merely a final score.

Before an expensive sweep use a few preselected known good, bad and confusing
cases, one or two settings, stage artifacts and a clear stop condition. This tests
operationalization; it is not independent confirmation of the scientific claim.
Scale only after the measurement can distinguish intended cases.

The result record needs purpose, setup, data/splits, parameters, metric, observed
result or `NOT RUN`, interpretation and next decision. Retain representative cases
when aggregates hide the failure. Visual evidence is conditional: use
[the visual contract](../../experiment-card-writer/references/visual_evidence_contract.md)
only when a plot or viewer answers a distinct question. Direct results do not wait
for viewer polish.
