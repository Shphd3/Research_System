# Problem Discovery

Use for a genuinely new or reopened question, not every result reply. Follow
[the kernel's question stage](../../../../docs/methodology/research_exploration_kernel.md#1-question-and-structure).

Name the affected system, observed limitation/cost, decision an answer changes,
consequence of leaving it unsolved, useful success and boundary of significance.
Inspect existing work before naming the unresolved question. A prescribed tool
may constrain the search; do not fabricate a problem to justify it.

Describe stability/change, locality, sparsity/repetition, capacity, information
availability, authority and costly errors before proposing an architecture.
For consequential structure record observed/conjectured status, source or missing
evidence, scope, mathematical representation, candidate mechanism, why it could
help, counterexample and separator. A conjecture is allowed; a circular prior
that merely renames the mechanism is not.

When the structure itself is unknown, propose a small observational or profiling
test of structural alternatives, not a full solution benchmark. Distinguish an
ill-posed question, low importance, absent structure and an ineffective mechanism.

Return a concise question and justified next uncertainty. The researcher activates
workstreams; do not create Focus, Anchor or a tree merely because an idea is possible.
