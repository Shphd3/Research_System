Project 2: Original Research Manuscript and Presentation

Project Requirements

·        Submit a 10-page research paper based on your ongoing research or undergraduate/master's thesis in CVPR format; the final manuscript should be prepared and submitted in LaTeX (.tex and .pdf ).

·        Prepare presentation slides and a poster for your project.

·        Deliver an in-class presentation of about 15 minutes, focusing on core contributions and innovations.

·        Written manuscript: 20%; in-class presentation: 25%.
