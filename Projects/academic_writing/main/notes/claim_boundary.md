# Claim Boundary Notes

## Strong Claim To Avoid

```text
MoE gating has been shown to form robust functional expert specialization.
```

This is too strong for the current evidence.

## Safer Claim

```text
The current work shows why route consistency is not enough. It separates weak
same-feature routing consistency from stronger functional specialization, and
it identifies route-utility binding as the next unresolved test.
```

## Presentation Boundary

In slides, separate:

```text
result: what was measured
interpretation: what the measurement suggests
claim: what is safe to say
boundary: what remains unproved
```

## Immediate Slide Decision

Use the presentation to argue for the diagnostic framework and current evidence
state, not for a finished architecture.
