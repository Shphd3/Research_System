Use this file to track papers worth remembering.

## Status Meaning

- `todo`: identified but not read
- `reading`: currently being read
- `done`: summary card finished

## Mainline Use

A paper updates the research mainline only if it changes one of:

- the core hypothesis
- the mathematical modeling
- the experimental design
- the interpretation of existing evidence
- the next most important decision
