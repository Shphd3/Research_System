# Figure sources

Figure A compares the fraction of hidden low-frequency SAE energy covered by two selected leaves on identical eligible positions. Both conditions use the same leaf pool. The unrestricted rule can choose leaves with the same or different parents. Coverage is averaged over eligible positions within each document, then over documents with an eligible position. The sample comprises 1,009 held-out prefixes and 15,309 eligible positions. Bars are point estimates. [Routing table](/data/250010109/MoE_Router/experiments/1126_sae_feature_clusters/night_overlap/table3_routing.csv)

Figure B compares Qwen-HD4-Oracle with A0 at exactly 0.5B, 1B and 3B effective training tokens. Points show Qwen minus A0 in nats. Error bars are 95% paired bootstrap intervals over the 1,024 held-out sequences of 2,048 targets. The archive also has oracle-only measurements at 0.25B and 2B. A0 has no matched checkpoint there. Both models use the same positions, target-word frequency bins and low-frequency SAE event weights.
