## Initial reasoning audit for the reviewer

The exact factorization \(K_{ij}=(c_i^\top c_j)(e_i^\top e_j)\) links input geometry and downstream error-signal alignment. It does not make \(e_i^\top e_j\) predictable from the input representations, because true targets and downstream sensitivities contribute. The current eligible result supports only: *the registered readers did not achieve the registered 5% held-out MSE reduction for this checkpoint, matrix, corpus and pairing scheme*. The conditional 95% upper bound is 0.002294. It does not prove zero mutual information, irreducible conditional variance, uselessness of a better reader, stable natural conflict groups, finite loss harm, or a benefit from isolating parameters. The 50-epoch training fits achieved lower TRAIN loss at later epochs, but DEV picked early B2 weights; that is a reason to inspect generalization as a rival, not a mechanism verdict. Positive/negative CONFIRM \(K\) counts were 508/516; sign variation alone is not a conflict taxonomy.

The finite bridge is an independent numerical limitation: DEV residual and source-loss guards mostly passed, but too many \(|\eta K|\) values were below \(20\delta_L\). No registered candidate qualified; the confirmation intervention was therefore correctly skipped. Structure was separately skipped by the main-result gate. Do not turn either skip into non-support evidence. The historical exposure ledger and single-shard document-start population bound external generalization; pair bootstrap conditions on fixed readers and corpus.
