Updated 2026-10-18. This project reading index is not a new Anchor, experimental design, or scientific approval. Existing results retain their original identities. The present audit asks only why an input containing a low-frequency feature does not reach its designated expert. Learning benefit remains unknown. Keep three distinctions: **statistical graph hierarchy h versus model layer; cross-layer decoder magnitude share p versus activation frequency p; designated ownership versus actual reception of an entire input.** Within-expert frequency q divides by positions received by that expert. Empty-expert q is NA, not zero.

This audit completed per-feature reception/frequency checks, score decomposition and limited virtual score interventions, fixed text cases, and limited cross-group co-occurrence bounds. It trained no experts and introduced no new scores or groups. C reuses the original 962-item TRAIN top-frequency-decile list; it is neither a new frequency≥50% definition nor a semantic category.

**Single priority decision, AI recommendation:** define whether routing should compare raw average magnitude or response relative to each group's own baseline, then freeze one calibration comparison that also checks costs to other frequency ranges. Its formula, reception requirements, and validation standards remain for discussion; none was executed here. Independent frequency, magnitude, and membership effects remain unresolved. Learning benefit and capacity improvement remain unknown.
