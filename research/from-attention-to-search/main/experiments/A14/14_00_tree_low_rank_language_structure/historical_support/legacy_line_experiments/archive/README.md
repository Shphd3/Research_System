# A14 Archived Operationalizations

These experiments are preserved for provenance and reproducibility. They are
not evidence for the candidate conditional local low-rank tree hypothesis.

| Archived folder | Why archived | Consult again when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [E02 gold syntax + PPMI](A14_E02_gold_syntax_ppmi/summary.md) | Averaged word co-occurrence vectors are a bag-of-words distributional proxy, not a parent-child compositional state. | A future study needs a non-neural distributional baseline. |
| [E03 pretrained tree geometry](A14_E03_pretrained_tree_geometry/summary.md) | Independent-span one-step prediction, child averaging, and cross-parent permutation do not instantiate recursive role-specific tree propagation. | Auditing readout anisotropy or reconstructing the lexical-containment failure. |
| [E04 causal hierarchy training](A14_E04_causal_hierarchy_training/summary.md) | The registered equal-capability guard admitted no pair, and the composition metric inherited the parent-content shortcut. | Designing a new controlled conditional-tree intervention with a valid capability surface. |
| [E05 same-parent split](A14_E05_same_parent_split_stability/summary.md) | The same-parent control fixed lexical containment, but its alternative boundaries were directionally imbalanced; E06 superseded its aggregate. | Reconstructing why direction balance became necessary. |
