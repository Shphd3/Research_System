## Result Snapshot

**Direct result:** the same four pre-fixed terminal parameter spaces are globally shared across layers, but none gains conditional-fine semantic enrichment from early to late depth. This rules out the strongest registered rival that the broad 2,048-dimensional decline merely averaged over a narrower deep-tail semantic destination.

**Registered verdict:** `SUPPORTED_DECOUPLED_TERMINAL_SKELETON`.

**AI-proposed bounded reading for researcher confirmation:** the deepest tail contains a stable cross-layer parameter-geometric component, but parameter stability is not a chronology of where increasingly fine late semantics enter. On this frozen set, the shared terminal geometry is more aligned with early conditional-fine attention-induced MLP-input increments than with late ones.

**One next decision:** confirm or correct that proposed knowledge update before A15_04's Anchor or a new problem focus is changed.

## Decision-Bearing Setup

- **Question:** does the old deep-tail parameter overlap remain decoupled from late semantic entry at terminal widths 128, 256, 512, and 2,048?
- **Treatment:** the last \(w\) native parameter eigenvectors, converted into an odd/even cross-fitted consensus space for each \(w\).
- **Control:** the q95 overlap of 512 random spaces of the same dimension.
- **Semantic statistic:** within-parent between-child covariance of the actual attention-induced MLP-input increment \(\Delta n\); no coarse, complexity, task, or Router statistic enters.
- **Semantic entry change:** late-layer median minus early-layer median of variance capture divided by the random dimension fraction \(w/4096\).
- **Held fixed:** Qwen3-8B, the original complete parameter bases, the eligible recovered raw \(\Delta n\), 512 records, and layers 1--35.

## Claim Boundary

**Can claim:** on this frozen 512-record set, terminal parameter spaces of widths 128/256/512/2,048 contain a cross-layer shared geometric component, while conditional-fine \(\Delta n\) enrichment in the same spaces decreases from early to late layers.

**Cannot claim:** the tail is unused, random, semantically empty, a functional null sink, task-irrelevant, causally inert, or unsuitable for nonlinear routing. Above-null parameter overlap also does not mean the terminal space is identical at every stage.
