## 2. Anchor Alignment

This experiment does not change A15_04's object or verdict. A15_04_E01 already established that the broad F9--F16 shared tail loses conditional-fine enrichment from early to late depth despite above-null global parameter overlap. The unresolved explanation is whether that decoupling is an artifact of using a 2,048-dimensional union or persists inside the previously measured deepest 128/256/512 directions.

The experiment may strengthen only this local explanatory clause: a terminal parameter geometry can be stable across layers without serving as the destination of increasing late conditional-fine variance. It may weaken that clause if a pre-fixed narrower width shows robust positive semantic entry.

## 3. Tested Hypothesis

**H-decoupling.** Across the four terminal widths fixed before this analysis, parameter geometry is globally shared above matched random, while conditional-fine semantic enrichment in that same cross-fitted geometry decreases rather than increases from early to late layers.

**Strongest rival — width averaging.** The broad F9--F16 decline averages together a narrower deep-tail semantic destination and other directions that decline. Under this rival, at least one of the old pre-fixed widths 128, 256, or 512 has a robust positive \(T_w\) even though \(T_{2048}<0\).

## 7. Geometry And Frozen Variants

For layer \(\ell\) and width \(w\), let \(V_{\ell,w}\) contain the last \(w\) native eigenvectors and \(P_{\ell,w}=V_{\ell,w}V_{\ell,w}^{\top}\).

Global cross-fitting uses layers 1--35:

\[
Q_w^{(-f)}=\operatorname{TopEig}_w\left(\frac{1}{|-f|}\sum_{j\in-f}P_{j,w}\right),
\]

where an evaluated odd layer uses the even-built space and vice versa. The four widths are nested but each consensus space is fitted directly at its own rank; a smaller result is never obtained by truncating a larger fitted consensus basis.

Stage diagnostics repeat the same construction inside early layers 1--12 and late layers 25--35. Cross-stage spaces use all early parameter projectors and evaluate only late semantic covariance, or use all late projectors and evaluate only early semantic covariance. Stage results are descriptive.

## 8. Objective, Optimization, And Precision

There is no objective or optimization. Parameter bases and recovered representations remain frozen. Consensus eigendecomposition and projection use FP32; overlap, traces, aggregation, and uncertainty use FP64. Every consensus basis must have normalized orthogonality error and eigensolver residual at most \(10^{-5}\).

## 9. Conditions, Randomness, And Source Reproduction

- widths: 128, 256, 512, 2,048;
- parameter overlap null: 512 Haar orthogonal draws, seed `15040201`;
- grouped bootstrap: 2,000 paired resamples, seed `15040202`;
- resampling units: parents and templates; the same resample is applied to every layer and width;
- expression halves: templates 1--2 and 3--4;
- layer splits: odd and even evaluated layers;
- source reproduction tolerance: absolute difference at most \(10^{-12}\) for all old native tail means at widths 128/256/512 and the broad 2,048 mean computed from the saved pair records.

Layer pairs, layers, directions, and nested widths are repeated measurements, not independent model samples.

## 10. Primary Metric And Frozen Analysis Plan

### 10.1 Parameter clause

For every width, report the old pairwise source mean \(A_w\), its dimension-normalized ratio \(A_w/(w/4096)\), the new global consensus overlap \(C_w\), and matched Haar q95. The parameter clause passes at width \(w\) only if the source reproduces and \(C_w>q95_w\).

### 10.2 Semantic clause

For each evaluated layer, project conditional-fine deviations into the opposite-parity \(Q_w\). Report capture, dimension-normalized enrichment, early median, late median, and

\[
T_w=\operatorname{median}_{\ell=25:35}e_{\ell,w}
-\operatorname{median}_{\ell=1:12}e_{\ell,w}.
\]

Use 2,000 paired parent/template bootstrap replicates to obtain a percentile 95% interval for every \(T_w\). The semantic non-destination clause passes at width \(w\) only if its 95% upper endpoint is below zero. Also report odd-only, even-only, design-half, and confirmation-half \(T_w\); all must be negative for the joint verdict.

To protect a conclusion that one of four widths positively contradicts the interpretation, require the Bonferroni-protected two-sided 98.75% lower endpoint above zero plus positive signs in all four splits. No width is selected after result access.

### 10.3 Joint verdict

| Verdict | Frozen rule | Allowed update |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `SUPPORTED_DECOUPLED_TERMINAL_SKELETON` | all four parameter clauses pass; all four semantic 95% upper endpoints are below zero; all split signs are negative | the tested deep terminal parameter geometry is stable but is not the destination of increasing late conditional-fine variance on this set |
| `WIDTH_LOCALIZED_SEMANTIC_ENTRY` | parameter clause passes and at least one width meets the protected positive rule | the broad-tail failure hid a pre-fixed narrower semantic-entry scale; do not generalize the non-destination interpretation across the terminal tail |
| `MIXED_RESOLUTION_OR_INSUFFICIENT` | neither rule is met, any required guard fails, or an interval/split is unstable | preserve the broad A15_04 Fail but do not close the narrower explanation |

This is a conjunctive four-width support rule. The widths are not averaged into one scalar and secondary diagnostics cannot rescue a failed clause.

## 11. Secondary Metrics And Mandatory Stage Diagnostics

For every width, report:

1. early-internal odd/even parameter overlap and median semantic enrichment;
2. late-internal odd/even parameter overlap and median semantic enrichment;
3. early-built parameter space evaluated on late semantics;
4. late-built parameter space evaluated on early semantics;
5. early-built versus late-built parameter-space overlap;
6. late absolute enrichment level, separated from the depth change \(T_w\).

These quantities may describe globally stable geometry, late formation, or stage redirection. They do not enter the joint verdict and cannot establish function.

## 12. Known Good, Known Bad, And Known Confusing Cases

- **Known good:** identical orthonormal bases have overlap 1; a semantic matrix fully inside a tested basis has capture 1.
- **Known bad:** independent random rank-\(w\) spaces center near \(w/4096\); duplicate semantic cells or incomplete 512-record cubes must stop.
- **Known confusing:** a space can have high parameter overlap and above-random semantic occupancy at both stages while \(T_w\le0\). That means semantic variance is present, not that it increasingly enters.
- **Known confusing:** nested widths are correlated. They are resolution checks, not four independent model replications.
