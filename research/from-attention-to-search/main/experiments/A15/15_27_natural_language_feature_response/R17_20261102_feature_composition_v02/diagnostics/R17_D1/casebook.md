# D1 exact casebook

The bilingual tables and verbatim model-input English texts, token IDs, native candidate scores and observed raw coordinates are in [casebook_cn.md](casebook_cn.md). It includes all8fixed D1_M05_0 fine conditions, original pre-fixed R17 errors/correct cases, dictionary-specific C/F responses and failed high-confidence examples. Chinese narration is explanatory; numbers and English inputs are identical. No case is a substitute for the frozen-panel statistics.

The explicit counterexample is D1_M05_0 invertible/no-answer/p0: MLPIN continuous/binary predict invertible with0.209771/0.315053, both wrong; corrected CC continuous0.642636 is correct but binary0.001928 is confidently wrong. This prevents treating binarization as a universal repair. H4 mixed-object passages remain unknown rather than forced into a fine class.
