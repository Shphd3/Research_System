## Decision Question

Do early A/B/AB routing experiments establish stable feature-level expert
specialization, or do token, position, context, target, and initialization
shortcuts explain the observed buckets?

## Evidence Contract

The registered bundle compares minimal and length-32 tasks, Router-factor
controls, expert-spectrum diagnostics, context-balanced source-token routing,
soft versus top-1 correction, initialization trajectories, route swaps, prior
decay, and Router refresh. Each child directory owns its exact setup and raw
metrics.

The bundle supports specialization only if route identity is stable across
shortcut controls and forced-expert utility identifies different useful expert
functions. Route purity, load balance, or expert spectra alone do not pass.

## Boundary

This is a retrospective protocol index over completed child experiments. It
does not authorize a new run or strengthen any child result.
