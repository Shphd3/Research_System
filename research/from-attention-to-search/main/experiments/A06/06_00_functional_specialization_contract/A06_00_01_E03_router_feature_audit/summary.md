# Summary: A06_00_01_E03 Router Feature Audit Bundle

The completed child experiments do not establish clean feature-level expert
specialization. The synthetic tasks are learned, but Router assignments are
better explained by token identity, position, context, target shortcuts, and
initialization history than by a stable rule/function partition.

The result is operationally useful: it defines observable collapse and
shortcut failures that later anchors must control. It is not positive evidence
of expert utility because a stable held-out forced-expert advantage was not
established.

The 12 child experiments and their statuses are indexed in
[`README.md`](README.md). Exact evidence remains in each child directory.

**Claim boundary:** synthetic labels and route purity cannot identify a useful
expert without counterfactual held-out loss.
