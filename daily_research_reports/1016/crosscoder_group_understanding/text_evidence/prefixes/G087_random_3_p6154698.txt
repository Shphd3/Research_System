There's nothing wrong with this: it's common practice in news headlines, and native speakers will find a well-worded sentence perfectly intelligible if the articles are removed. You must be careful about your choice of words, however, to avoid negatively affecting the clarity of your writing. It's probably not a problem as long as the text is relatively short, and presentation slides shouldn't have a lot of text, anyway.

Also, if you're going to remove any of the articles, you should probably remove all of them, otherwise it looks awkward, and might be interpreted as an error.
