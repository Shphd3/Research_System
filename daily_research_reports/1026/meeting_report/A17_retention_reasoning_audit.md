# Reasoning audit of the current retention report

[Full audit in Chinese](A17_retention_reasoning_audit_cn.md) · [Revised brief](meeting_brief_A17_retention_reasoning_revision.md).

Distinguish missing exposition from missing research. Existing source evidence already covers Top-16 efficacy and new-learning timing, candidate probability sensitivity with concentrated effects, actual-displacement implementation and finite-step limitations, D magnitude controls, matched new-learning qualification, conditional rule confusion, six-state immediate F counterfactuals, and full Adam-moment exchange. Add these to the report rather than rerun them.

Still unresolved: C's functional necessity/coverage and cross-task reuse; first versus second Adam moments; how local update effects accumulate into the endpoint difference; pure frequency at fixed old-objective weight. These stronger claims require additional identifying evidence. Small perturbations, shared parameter involvement, or a hybrid optimizer state do not automatically establish a reusable module or a long-term causal pathway.

The revised brief corrects the mathematical object (Adam displacement, not raw gradient), first-order versus finite-loss guarantee, whole-matrix training versus coefficient protection, endpoint qualification versus speed, and different-trajectory endpoint comparisons versus same-state intervention. All original results and contracts are unchanged. No experiment was run for this document audit.
