## Newly Completed Diagnostic Controls

### 1. `linear_no_center`

- Role: collapse control
- Key numbers:
  - `Round0 Avg = 0.4253`
  - `CR@0.5 = 1.0000`
  - `Mean SI = 0.8063`

Interpretation:

- Its apparently perfect routing consistency is pathological rather than desirable.
- The simultaneously high `SI` and weak downstream average indicate collapse-like routing, not meaningful specialization.

### 2. `linear_nocenter_lb001`

- Role: explicit load-balancing control
- Key numbers:
  - `Round0 Avg = 0.4361`
  - `CR@0.5 = 0.9529`
  - `Mean SI = 0.6714`

Interpretation:

- Explicit load balancing improves over the raw no-centering baseline.
- However, it still does not surpass the best geometry-aware variants on the overall robustness/overlap tradeoff.

## Paper-Level Consequence

- the checkpoint audit explicitly includes both a collapse control (`linear_no_center`) and a load-balancing control (`linear_nocenter_lb001`);
- therefore the empirical gains of the best low-rank / hybrid routes cannot be explained away as balancing regularization alone.
