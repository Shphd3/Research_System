## Keep the Mainline in Five Sentences

1. All-position NTP eventually supervises the answer; MTP's candidate advantage
   is earlier direct credit, not permanent answer blindness.
2. Token distance is not the causal property. Local insufficiency, delayed
   counterfactual dependence, and multi-evidence composition are.
3. Earlier formation does not establish transport or standard-head causal use.
4. A favorable workload resembles a deterministic state relay, not the
   abstract `code` label; either code or natural language may or may not have
   those properties.
5. No current carrier is valid. Freeze definitions and validity gates before
   any NTP/MTP comparison.
