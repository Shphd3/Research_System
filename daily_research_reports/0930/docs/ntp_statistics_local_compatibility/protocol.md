## 10. Primary Metric And Frozen Analysis Plan

For stratum \(h=(y,s,t)\), quartet \(k\), compute

\[
Z_{hk}=\langle G^F_{s,hk}-G^F_{t,hk},G^R_{s,hk}-G^R_{t,hk}\rangle_F,
\qquad \widehat T=\frac1{24v_0}\sum_{h=1}^{24}\bar Z_h.
\]

Use four FP64 rank-one Gram products; never form 192 dense covariance matrices. Preserve these terms separately for the signed-secondary decomposition in §11. Positive T alone cannot establish any negative group cross-effect. Each \(Z\) has units gradient-squared; \(\widehat T\) is dimensionless. Observational independence unit is the quartet of four source records, conditional on the documented record assumption. Targets, domain pairs and the two subcategories are balanced by construction.

With \(n_h=8\), sample variance \(s_h^2\), define \(a_h=s_h^2/n_h\),

\[
\mathrm{SE}=\frac{\sqrt{\sum_h a_h}}{24v_0},\quad
\nu=\frac{(\sum_h a_h)^2}{\sum_h a_h^2/(n_h-1)},\quad
\mathrm{CI}_{95}=\widehat T\pm t_{0.975,\nu}\mathrm{SE}.
\]

This is a proposed approximate stratified scalar Welch interval, not an automatic coverage guarantee. At least 5,000 simulated independent repetitions per registered case must precede primary unsealing: zero means with isotropic covariance; zero means with rank-one anisotropy; unequal domain variances; Student-t(5) finite-moment noise; target-only shared means; nonzero recurring contrasts; reversed source-pool contrasts. Include true scaled effects 0,0.005,0.01,0.02 where applicable. Require empirical 95% coverage in [0.93,0.97], absolute bias ≤0.1 median SE, and false support ≤0.035 at the 0.01 boundary. Failed calibration gives `INSUFFICIENT_METRIC`; it does not permit selecting another interval after seeing natural results.

One primary test, no multiplicity adjustment needed for its single interval; all secondary intervals are explicitly exploratory. No outlier deletion or clipping; report extremes. Missing/nonfinite events, zero total variance, or a required cell falling below 12 endpoints stops primary analysis. R gradients remain sealed until candidate target/endpoint manifests, v0, analysis code hash and calibration receipt are frozen. F is not used to choose a favorable contrast. No adaptive collection or optional stopping.
