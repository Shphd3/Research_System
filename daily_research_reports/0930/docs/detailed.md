## 1. Objects and inference level

An **event** is one document's first 256 tokens and its true 257th-token target. At the last prefix position, \(c\in\mathbb R^{2048}\) enters the last attention output matrix \(W\in\mathbb R^{1024\times2048}\), \(a=Wc\), and \(e=\partial\ell/\partial a\) is the derivative of pure full-vocabulary NTP cross-entropy. The single-event matrix gradient is \(G=ec^\top\); the exact pairwise gradient inner product is \(K_{ij}=(c_i^\top c_j)(e_i^\top e_j)\). Its sign describes a first-order cross-update tendency under scalar SGD. The registered readers receive \(c_i,c_j\), not \(e\), true targets, loss, or \(K\) at inference. \(Q\) is a dimensionless held-out prediction score. A finite loss change \(I\) would require a qualified temporary update and is not inferred from \(K\) alone.

The causal and statistical levels are therefore separate: exact gradient factorization is a mathematical identity; held-out \(Q\) tests statistical extractability under the readers; DEV temporary updates test step calibration; no long-term shared/separate training or Router intervention was done.

## 4. Registered metric and evidence lineage

On raw \(K\), \(Q=1-\sum(K-q_*)^2/\sum(K-m_{\rm TRAIN})^2\). The constant uses only TRAIN labels. The 95% interval is the linear 2.5/97.5 percentile of 2,000 PCG64 draws of 1,024 CONFIRM pair indices with replacement, seed 20260931; selected weights stay fixed. Support requires the interval lower bound >0.05, non-support upper bound <0.05, otherwise insufficient. The denominator and target variance must exceed \(100\delta_K^2\). Observed baseline MSE 0.638792 and variance 0.638767 greatly exceed that floor (\(100\delta_K^2\approx3.31\times10^{-10}\)). All \(K\) values are finite and are retained, including extremes.

## 7. Reasoning audit and boundary

The exact identity \(K=(c_i^\top c_j)(e_i^\top e_j)\) says the input geometry matters *along with* downstream error-signal alignment. It does not imply \(e_i^\top e_j\), which depends on the actual targets and downstream state, is predictable from \(c_i,c_j\). The current result directly rejects the registered 5% score for these readers and fixed setting. It does not separately distinguish weak conditional information from finite reader/sample/optimization capacity. The very different TRAIN fit at late epochs versus DEV selection is consistent with generalization limits, but is not a causal diagnosis. DEV score was tiny before CONFIRM and is not an independent second confirmation.

The strongest still-live rival to “matrix input has weak predictive information” is “a better specified reader, data scale, or conditioning variable could extract information the registered readers missed.” Its answer requires a *new* prospective contract and untouched confirmation set, not post-hoc re-fitting on the current CONFIRM. The observed near-half positive/negative signs show the local first-order label varies; they do not show persistent task conflict or net harmful finite loss. The DEV Taylor residual guard passing does not license a CONFIRM finite-loss statement because the first-order signal was too often below the resolution guard. No structural PCA/SAE inference is available because those readers were not run. No isolated representation intervention or shared-versus-separate training comparison was performed.

**Maximum supported claim:** under this single checkpoint, matrix, document-start corpus, fixed data and reader families, the predeclared 5% reduction in held-out \(K\) MSE did not occur and the conditional 95% upper bound is 0.002294. This is an operational negative result, not a general “representations cannot predict compatibility” theorem. Human interpretation, Focus transition, and the next scientific priority remain `Unknown`; an AI proposal to discuss capacity versus information is not execution approval.
