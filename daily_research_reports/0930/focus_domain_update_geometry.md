## Question And Boundary

At a fixed S7 state, do four predefined natural-text domains have reproducible
local update differences beyond the pooled mean? How do SVD and trained SAE
activation relate to those differences? Understand update geometry before
deciding on another representation-only predictor.

Scope remains the final attention matrix, pure next-token loss and a historical
academic panel. No semantic causality, representation-only prediction success,
finite-step natural-text harm, isolation benefit or router gain follows.

## Hypothesis And Discrimination

Fit domain means should describe heldout gradients better than the pooled mean.
Recurring activity need not imply recurring mean updates; weak domain differences
and high-dimensional estimation error remain unresolved alternatives.
Qualified reproducible improvement supports the mean relation, qualified precise
non-improvement weakens it, and unqualified inference remains insufficient.
Spectral/SAE outcomes do not substitute for the primary comparison.
