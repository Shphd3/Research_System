# Complete analysis and researcher-first review

Apply this contract to a requested complete experiment/analysis matrix. For
first delivery of the researcher's own newly completed experiments, default to
observations before AI mechanism judgments, following the researcher's standing
preference; use a combined pass only when explicitly requested. Preserve the user's current scope and budget;
this reference grants no execution authority. Narrow factual answers do not
need the full delivery format.

## Coverage and independent dependencies

Freeze a compact manifest of assets, data/family splits, seeds, factors, tensor
interfaces, layers, metrics, fitting and selection rules, budget and stops.
Maintain every requested task and optional dependent subtask in one coverage
ledger. Each final row is `DONE_VALID`, `DONE_EXPLORATORY`, `INVALID`, `FAILED`
or `BLOCKED`, with evidence, counts, missing dependency and bounded repair.
Do not delete unsuccessful conditions or substitute an ineligible measurement.
One failed dictionary or a weak behavior result does not cancel independent
native or descriptive analyses. Full completion requires all applicable work;
report remaining blockers explicitly. A ledger audit is not a scientific verdict.

For composition, distance and frequency analyses define each separately:
task composition depth is necessary relation count, not internal reasoning
steps; distance uses actual token positions; reference-corpus frequency is a
proxy, controlled training exposure is a different intervention, and token or
dictionary-feature occurrence cannot silently replace either. Distinguish
granularity, common/specific, sparsity and the rank of a named matrix.

## Separate fitting protocols and three recorded audits

Separate readout training, statistical fitting (including PCA), new dictionary
training, controlled-exposure model training, and common/remaining update
experiments. Authorization of one does not authorize another. For each requested
fitting/learning protocol save three concise, distinct audit records:

1. Scientific identification: question, rival, changed/held-fixed objects,
   observable, permissible claim and identification limits.
2. Data/statistics: generation, independent families, splits, exposure/marginals,
   leakage, controls, uncertainty, multiplicity and matched budgets.
3. Implementation/numerics: actual trainable parameters/count, freeze evidence,
   gradients or justified non-applicability for closed-form statistics, objective,
   optimizer/schedule/precision, counting, save/resume, calibration and bounded
   smoke outputs. Audit-only unapproved training stays NOT RUN.

Use the [training observability contract](training_observability_contract.md)
for actual training. Do not invent LM-loss/token quantities for statistical
fitting or classifier labels; declare their applicable counters and exclusions.
Use numerical and no-op checks that protect interpretation, without turning
routine work into redundant approval stages. Repair within authorized limits;
do not tune indefinitely or widen tolerances after observing an effect.

## First delivery: measurements before mechanism judgment

Open the complete factual explanation with exactly these three analysis paths,
once for the whole report:

1. Behavior and factor controls: under which conditions predictions change.
2. Representations and layerwise organization: what is observed or readable.
3. Functional interventions and quality: whether changes affect predictions and
   whether measurements are credible.

Use a layered factual delivery: Summary explains the question, key comparison
and decisive measurements; Detailed explains the actual steps and why controls
are needed; linked appendices retain the complete per-item record and audit.
Do not put the full ledger on the first reading surface. Across these linked
records, explain every requested item in its registered order: purpose and need;
real input/control/operation; raw values, baseline, difference, units, counts
and uncertainty; first-use terms; every plot/table encoding; validity and limits.
At first use of a term give its Chinese name, plain meaning, experiment-specific
object, computation or plot-reading rule, and what it cannot establish. A
glossary alone cannot replace these local definitions. Follow a real example
through the computation and include a real contrary/failed case where available;
state when none exists. Never create a fictitious result for an unrun item.

Keep observations, numerical/data eligibility and missingness visible. Defer
support/refutation of mechanisms, architecture rankings, direction changes and
next scientific choices until researcher review; do not hide them in a summary,
appendix or suggested next step. Keep scientific conclusion pending. This delay
does not stop other authorized frozen-plan analyses or reporting quality errors.
End with the coverage table and one or two understanding questions, then wait.
Long explanations belong in the linked report; chat supplies the reading order.

## Second delivery after review

Restate and correct the researcher's observations, then connect observation,
hypothesis supported/weakened, live rivals, evidence boundary and one proposed
minimal next test. Label AI judgments and suggestions; do not attribute them to
the researcher or change scientific state without authorization.
