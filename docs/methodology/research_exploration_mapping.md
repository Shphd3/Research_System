## Intentional Non-Imports

Upstream four/five-section `design.md` and automatic research map are replaced by
local eight-section Anchor and typed existing relationships. Full round history
stays in Detailed, not copied into each design. Root Focus remains additional
human judgment, not another experiment ledger.

LaTeX build/toolchain, installer, viewer-server script and optional dashboard
infrastructure are not imported: they are peripheral tooling, not missing research
methodology. A requested paper/viewer may use its own tools later. No automatic
service, extra model invocation, external repository edit or historical result
migration is authorized by this integration.

## Compression Basis And Limits

Follow the [Astra refactor](../research_system_astra_review_20260926_cn.md) and
[Astra/Sol compatibility record](../ai_workflow_compatibility_20260926_cn.md):
one source per rule, task-conditioned references, same authority across models,
no duplicate approval, factual completion with unknown judgment, and separate
entrypoint byte counts from actual read-path cost. Those records are historical
evidence, not a new Astra/Sol test performed in this refactor.
