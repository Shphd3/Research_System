# Research Exploration Method Kernel

Adapted from the pinned upstream listed in [mapping](research_exploration_mapping.md)
and [notices](../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). This is a reasoning contract,
not an installed skill, extra approval layer or required output template.
AGENTS owns authority; [task owners](../skills.md) own artifacts. Read only the
applicable stage; reuse verified work rather than restarting the sequence.

The purpose, question, hypothesis, controls, evidence, interpretation and bounded
answer must form an explicit argument. Section order, fixed word counts and
unchanged length do not establish validity. Use the shared
[writing examples and acceptance checks](../report_writing.md#check-the-text-itself):
keep the first reading clear, permit necessary modest growth, and layer full
derivations/audits. No phenomenon becomes a mechanism or discussed test a result.

## 1. Question And Structure

Start with the phenomenon, genuine uncertainty, importance and scope. Identify
the limitation, its cost, the decision an answer changes, and the significance
boundary.
A prescribed method constrains the work; it does not supply its purpose.

Describe what stays fixed, what varies, locality, repetition, capacity, timing
of available information and costly failure. Priors are falsifiable claims about
this system or data process, not names of tools, splits or validation rules.
Mark structure observed or conjectured. When structure is unknown, an
observational/profiling test may precede a solution mechanism.
For formal questions state mathematical assumptions without inventing world
claims. Evidence may be proof, counterexample, observation, intervention or a
system comparison, as appropriate to the claim and stage.

## 2. Explanation And Mathematical Model

First inspect existing named derivations, Anchors and result records. Not shown
in a report does not mean not done. Separate model, empirical, presentation and
decision-bridge gaps, and state the inspected scope when retrieval is incomplete.

Connect scientific objects, variables/state, model mapping, objective or
observable, intervention/update and consequence. Define admissible states,
constraints, permitted information and the output being explained. Distinguish
exact derivation, local approximation, modeling assumption, empirical proposition
and interpretation. A constructible numerical model is valid; closed-form
solutions are not compulsory. Fixed diagnostics need an evaluation question,
not an invented training loss. Unsettled links may remain research questions.

For fitted models, additionally specify observed/latent/learned/output variables,
exact objective, units/aggregation, constraint enforcement, shortcuts and collapse,
supervision provenance, and operational/train/development/confirmation roles.
No evaluation-only truth may leak through pseudo-targets, selection or fitting.

A bounded result does not settle a broader design choice. Name the comparison
or assumption needed to bridge levels; do not require future-stage closure.

For open questions, try the smallest explanation that connects the observed
tension, explain what it adds beyond a credible rival, and derive a prediction
that could separate them. Retain the strongest valid conditional model when a
broader claim fails. Missing identification can motivate this exploration;
it is not by itself a reason to withhold all explanation. Use the shared
[review mode](../research_analysis_review_contract.md#review-mode).

### Mechanism Bridges And Operational Equivalence

For a consequential unexplained pattern, look for a useful connection to an
established theory: for example effective sample size, gradient moments,
information constraints, capacity or optimization dynamics. Map the actual
research objects to the theory's quantities and assumptions; explain which
observations this connects and derive a prediction the original account lacked.
A shared name or analogy is not a mechanism bridge. Check applicability and
relevant primary sources when needed; if no suitable bridge is found, say so.
Explore only the one or two links that advance the current question, without
forcing a theory survey or a fixed response template.

When saying “equivalent to,” “effective batch” or “effective capacity,” specify
the reference system, task/direction/parameter block, matched conditions, metric
of equivalence, breakdown conditions and new testable prediction. Equivalence
is relative to that metric and reference, not an identity of whole systems.
Keep actual token count, target-related token count, independent sample count
and reference-equivalent batch distinct. Correlated samples, weighting and loss
normalization affect the mapping. A scalar signal-to-noise match need not match
the signed mean update, other directions, optimizer state, training trajectory,
generalization or cost. Ratios such as expert count divided by active experts
are conditional model predictions, never universal batch multipliers.

## 3. Mechanism Choice And Computation

For a material prior-art choice explain problem, input-to-output computation,
why it exploits the stated structure, demonstrated scope/cost, supervision,
hidden teachers, failure modes and compatibility. End in adopt, adapt, baseline,
external-only, reject or defer. Search to change a decision, not to collect names.

Beside each consequential stage give Goal, How and Why: prior or structural
assumption -> mathematical relation -> operation -> intended behavior -> residual
failure. Label engineering choices honestly. An independent implementer must
not invent outcome-changing settings: freeze them, freeze a sweep/selection rule,
or mark the missing choice. Include input/output, architecture when applicable,
training, inference/refusal, and evaluation. Commands/resources remain in Protocol.

## 4. Distinguishing Evidence

Map each decision-bearing mechanism term to its implemented quantity, comparison,
held-fixed variables, observable and remaining confound. Independent intervention
is one strategy, not a fiction that coupled factors can always be varied alone.
A justified joint design may identify only a joint effect; otherwise state
compatibility, not isolated causation. Prediction, attribution and intervention
are separate claims. Implementation tests of identities are not novel evidence.

Before a costly sweep use a few known good, bad and confusing cases, one or two
settings, stage artifacts and a stop condition. Freeze schemas, splits, criteria,
selection rules and permitted information before confirmation. Each important
arrow needs inspectable input/output/failure evidence, not just a final metric.
Keep single-observation, change and aggregate evidence distinct.

Report direct results before polish. Diagnose invalid measurement, implementation,
model, prior/boundary, question/importance or insufficient evidence at the supported
level. A failed proxy does not refute every lawful mechanism. Check finite-step
remainders against the component actually claimed, not merely the total effect.

## 5. Update, Refine And Explain

Return eligible evidence to the affected claim/assumption and bounded answer,
then its parent obligation. State what each supporting, non-supporting or
insufficient outcome changes, leaves open, and makes worth checking next.
Distinguish refinement, necessary dependency, chosen route and coupled questions.
A failed proxy or one route's missing structure does not block all other routes;
justify necessity before imposing a gate. For coupled questions state the joint
prediction, identification limit and separator. Do not pre-build inactive trees.
Activation and scientific judgment remain human-owned; factual work may continue.

Keep one preserved round history and derived rolling summary. Audit a goal when
repeated failures or unequal error costs expose a metric/purpose mismatch; never
silently redefine success. Close only a settled bounded question.

A reader must understand the question, assumptions, changed object, shortest
mechanism bridge, tested relation, result and remaining uncertainty. Preserve
source disagreements. Use figures only for a distinct evidential question;
state units, scales, alternatives and boundary. Math-source checks verify neither
rendering nor scientific reasoning.

## Proportional Completion

Check applicable obligations; repair the smallest material gap. Do not force
inactive stages, reply headings or redundant approval. When reasoning rules
change, use small cross-domain [scenarios](research_exploration_regression_cases.md)
to review actual decisions and explanations. Structure and self-review do not
establish independent model reliability or cross-domain scientific validity.
