The removed children had only a clause and brief verdict; their unique experiment records remain under their original IDs, now linked to the self-contained parent Anchor. `A06_00_03` had no standalone experiment; its real-text proxy guard is retained in A06_00.

Covariance rank is a proxy for spread; retention of added readout is a different construct; neither demonstrates Router utility. Its existing Protocols and E06 results link to this Anchor, so splitting or deleting it would require a researcher-confirmed scientific ownership decision. The current form should not be read as one mechanistic proof.

This separates “concentration improves N8 readout” from the rival that training history changes shared/scale directions without improving domain distinction. It does not identify a coefficient cause.
