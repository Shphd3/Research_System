\section{Analysis: Mean-Dominated Anisotropy in Large Language Models}

Prior work established that large language model training
exhibits persistent anisotropy across activations, gradients, and parameters.
In this section, we dissect this phenomenon empirically and theoretically.
Our goal is to answer three precise questions:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
    \item Does a coherent mean bias exist across layers and training stages?
    \item Does this mean bias dominate activation outliers?
    \item Does this mean bias dominate optimizer second-moment statistics?
\end{enumerate}
